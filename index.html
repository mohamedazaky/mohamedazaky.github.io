About me

I'm Mohamed, a Python developer based in Cairo. I work on the two things growing businesses need most: understanding their data, and running their operations on systems that actually fit them.

I build cloud ERP systems for trading and distribution businesses, covering sales, purchasing, inventory, warehouses, accounting and treasury in one place. Owners see stock, cash and customer balances live from any device, get alerted before items run out, and stop losing track of money in side spreadsheets and old desktop software.
